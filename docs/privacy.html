Privacy Policy

Kitchen Table Trivia, an Alexa skill. Effective November 9, 2026.

What the skill stores

Linked to the anonymous user ID that Alexa assigns your account for this skill:

  • Player names you say, like "Sam". Use a first name or a nickname; the skill only needs something to call each player.
  • For each player: whether they get easier questions, their points for this week and last week, their streak, and today's answer.
  • Which bonus-round questions your household has already heard, so they don't repeat soon.
  • If you turn on personalization for this skill and have an Alexa voice profile: the anonymous person ID Alexa sends for the recognized speaker, linked to the player name that speaker gave. This only lets the skill greet you by name; it never receives your voice profile, recordings, or real name.

The skill also reads your device's time zone from Alexa settings, so "today's question" changes at your local midnight. It does not store it.

The skill never receives your email address, contacts, location, or voice recordings. Your interactions with Alexa are handled by Amazon under Amazon's privacy notice; you can review and delete them in the Alexa app.

Where it's kept and who sees it

In the skill's database on Amazon Web Services. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to build a profile.

Deleting your data

  • "Alexa, ask Kitchen Table Trivia to remove Sam" deletes one player and their scores.
  • "Alexa, ask Kitchen Table Trivia to reset the game" deletes every player, score, and streak for your household, after you confirm.
  • Turning off personalization for the skill in the Alexa app stops Alexa from sending a person ID.

You can also email the address below.

Purchases

Kitchen Table Trivia Premium is sold and billed by Amazon. The skill learns only whether your account is subscribed; it never sees payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in the Alexa app or by saying "Alexa, ask Kitchen Table Trivia to cancel my subscription."

Operational logs

Errors are logged by the skill's hosting (Amazon Web Services) for troubleshooting. These logs contain request types and error messages.

Children

Kitchen Table Trivia is a game for households and is not directed to children. Younger family members can play with a grown-up's account, and the skill offers easier questions for them. Please use a first name or nickname for every player.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. Questions: feedback@hydroengineer.dev.
