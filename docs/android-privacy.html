This page covers the Android app only. The Alexa skill has its own privacy policy.

What the app stores, and where

Everything stays on your phone, in the app's private storage: the player names you type (use a first name or a nickname), whether each player gets easier questions, their points, streaks, today's answers, and which bonus questions you've already played. None of it is uploaded. The developer never receives it.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Deleting your data

Remove a player, or reset everything, in the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all of it. Device backup is turned off for this app, so the data is not copied to your Google account.

Purchases

Supper Table Trivia Premium is a one-time in-app purchase sold and billed by Google Play. The app asks Google Play only whether you own it; it never sees your name, email address, or payment details. Google handles the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy.

No ads, no analytics, no accounts

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. There is no sign-in and no account. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, and nothing is used to build a profile.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes
